Birthday Assistant V4

Ask me anything about birthdays!

Ask questions about birthdays, relationships or places. 


    


    
    


      
How V4 Works


      

        V4 uses an LLM to understand natural-language questions
        and retrieve factual answers from Excel.
      


      


        

          
1

          
Intent and Entity Extraction

          

            The LLM understands the user's question and
            identifies the intent, person, month and relationship.
          

        


        

          
2

          
Validation and Grounding

          

            Extracted names and other details are checked
            against the available spreadsheet records.
          

        


        

          
3

          
Excel Query Service

          

            The query service searches the Excel data
            and retrieves matching birthday information.
          

        


        

          
4

          
Answer

          

            The assistant displays the result using
            information from the spreadsheet.
